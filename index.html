GET TO KNOW OUR SCHOOL

A place where learning shapes the future.

Taguig National High School, formerly known as Taguig Municipal High School, is one of the educational institutions that has played an important role in the development of education in Taguig City.

Established on January 8, 1980, as Fort Bonifacio College Annex, the institution eventually developed into an independent school serving generations of learners.

Today, the school continues to uphold quality, inclusive, and learner-centered education. Its guiding philosophy, "Looking Beyond the Horizon," represents its commitment to helping students pursue opportunities and aspire toward a better future.

OUR PURPOSE

Our Vision and Mission

The guiding principles that shape our school community and the future of every learner.

Our Vision

We dream of Filipinos who passionately love their country and whose values and competencies enable them to realize their full potential and contribute meaningfully to building the nation.


As a learner-centered public institution, the Department of Education continuously improves itself to better serve its stakeholders.

Our Mission

To protect and promote the right of every Filipino to quality, equitable, culture-based, and complete basic education where:

  • Students learn in a child-friendly, safe, and motivating environment.
  • Teachers facilitate learning and nurture every learner.
  • Administrators and staff ensure a supportive learning environment.
  • Families and communities actively engage in developing lifelong learners.
